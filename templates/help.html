Quick notes before you calculate.

Attendance Calculator

  • Enter total classes held so far
  • Enter how many you attended
  • You'll see your current attendance %
  • Below 75% → shows classes needed in a row to recover
  • Above 75% → shows how many you can still skip

Internal Marks Calculator

  • Enter Series Test 1 out of 40 (or leave blank)
  • Enter Series Test 2 out of 40 (or leave blank)
  • Each test is scaled out of 10
  • Total internal shown out of 20
  • You can enter just one test if that's all you've taken
